# Privacy Policy – InspectFold

Effective Date: February 2026

InspectFold ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

---

## 1. Overview

InspectFold is a paid inspection and reporting application.  
The App does not require user accounts and does not collect personal data for tracking, advertising, or analytics purposes.

---

## 2. Data Collection

InspectFold does NOT:

- Require account registration
- Collect personal data for analytics
- Track users across apps or websites
- Sell or share user data with third parties
- Use third-party advertising SDKs

The App operates fully offline.

---

## 3. Data Stored on Device

All data created within InspectFold is stored locally on your device. This includes:

- Workspaces
- Inspection templates
- Notes
- Photos attached to inspections
- Captured signatures
- Generated PDF reports
- Profile information entered by the user

This data is not transmitted to external servers.

If you delete the App, locally stored data may be removed from your device.

---

## 4. Camera and Photo Library Access

InspectFold may request access to:

- Camera – to capture photos for inspection reports
- Photo Library – to attach existing images to inspection reports

These permissions are used solely for inspection documentation purposes and are not used for data collection or tracking.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

InspectFold does not automatically share any data.

Users may choose to manually share generated PDF reports using iOS sharing features. Any sharing action is initiated and controlled entirely by the user.

---

## 6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, data protection depends on your device security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, etc.).

InspectFold does not maintain remote servers or cloud databases.

---



## 7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if features change in future versions of the App. Updates will be reflected with a revised effective date.
